# Singapore Travel Guide (Wikivoyage)

## Overview & Geography
Singapore is an island city-state located at the southern tip of the Malay Peninsula in Southeast Asia. It consists of one main island and 63 satellite islands. Due to its strategic position, Singapore has developed into a global commerce, finance, and transport hub, known for cleanliness, safety, efficiency, and lush urban greenery ("City in a Garden").

## Major Districts & Neighbourhoods
* **Marina Bay & Downtown Core**: The modern financial and entertainment heart. Home to Marina Bay Sands, the Singapore Flyer, Merlion Park, Esplanade – Theatres on the Bay, and futuristic skyscrapers.
* **Chinatown**: A historic ethnic enclave featuring the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Sri Mariamman Temple (Singapore's oldest Hindu temple), traditional shophouses, medicinal tea stores, and lively souvenir street markets.
* **Little India**: A vibrant cultural district along Serangoon Road. Famous for colorful sari shops, fragrant spice merchants, flower garland stalls, the 24-hour shopping mall Mustafa Centre, and the historic Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple.
* **Kampong Glam (Arab Street & Haji Lane)**: The Malay-Muslim historic precinct centered around the majestic Sultan Mosque. Haji Lane is famous for narrow alleys, vibrant street murals, indie boutiques, hipster cafes, and textile stores.
* **Sentosa Island**: A resort island south of the mainland featuring sandy beaches (Palawan, Siloso, Tanjong), Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, Adventure Cove Waterpark, and luxury beachfront hotels.
* **Civic District & Orchard Road**: The Civic District holds the National Gallery Singapore and National Museum. Orchard Road is Singapore's premier shopping boulevard, with massive multi-story air-conditioned shopping malls spanning over 2 kilometers.

## Local Transportation Guide
* **MRT (Mass Rapid Transit)**: The MRT is the fastest, cleanest, and most convenient way to travel across Singapore. Trains run roughly from 05:30 to midnight daily at intervals of 2–5 minutes.
  * **Fares & Payment**: You can tap in and out directly using contactless Visa/Mastercard credit/debit cards or Apple Pay / Google Wallet. Foreign card administrative fees may apply.
  * Alternatively, buy a contactless **EZ-Link card** (available at MRT station Passenger Service Centres for SGD 10, including SGD 5 stored value) or a **Singapore Tourist Pass (STP)** offering unlimited travel for 1 day (SGD 10), 2 days (SGD 16), or 3 days (SGD 20), plus a refundable SGD 10 deposit.
* **Buses**: Public buses run by SBS Transit, SMRT, Tower Transit, and Go-Ahead complement the MRT, reaching areas not directly served by trains. Use the same contactless card or EZ-Link card. Always tap when boarding AND tap again when exiting.
* **Taxis & Ride-Hailing**: Metered taxis (ComfortDelGro) are readily available at taxi stands. Ride-hailing apps **Grab**, **Gojek**, and **Tada** are widely used. Surcharges apply during peak hours (06:00-09:30 and 18:00-midnight) and for trips originating from Changi Airport.

## Culture, Etiquette & Local Laws
* **Clean & Green Regulations**: Singapore enforces strict fines for littering, jaywalking, eating or drinking on public transport (MRT and buses), and smoking outside designated yellow-box zones. Chewing gum import and sale is prohibited.
* **Tipping Culture**: Tipping is not customary in Singapore. Most restaurants and hotels automatically add a 10% service charge and prevailing Goods and Services Tax (GST) to the bill.
* **Religious Sites**: Dress modestly when visiting mosques and temples; remove your shoes before entering, and cover your shoulders and knees.

## Food, Dining & Hawker Centres
Singapore is internationally celebrated as a culinary capital. The heart of Singaporean dining lies in its UNESCO-inscribed **Hawker Culture**.
* **Iconic Hawker Centres**:
  * **Maxwell Food Centre (Chinatown)**: Famous for Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice.
  * **Lau Pa Sat (Downtown)**: Renowned for its evening outdoor "Satay Street" with open-air charcoal grills.
  * **Old Airport Road Food Centre**: Beloved by locals for Char Kway Teow, Hokkien Mee, and Rojak.
  * **Tekka Centre (Little India)**: Best for authentic Indian curries, Biryani, and Roti Prata with Teh Tarik.
  * **Chinatown Complex Food Centre**: One of Singapore's largest hawker centres, offering cheap Michelin-starred and Bib Gourmand dishes.
* **Must-Try Local Dishes**:
  * **Hainanese Chicken Rice**: Poached or roasted chicken served over fragrant oily rice cooked in chicken broth, pandan, ginger, and garlic, accompanied by chili sauce.
  * **Chili Crab & Black Pepper Crab**: Fresh mud crabs smothered in a sweet, savory, and mildly spicy tomato-egg gravy, eaten with deep-fried mantou buns.
  * **Laksa (Katong Laksa)**: Spicy coconut milk noodle soup flavored with dried shrimp, fishcakes, cockles, and laksa leaves.
  * **Kaya Toast & Soft-Boiled Eggs**: The classic Singapore breakfast — crisp charcoal-toasted bread slathered with coconut-egg jam (kaya) and cold butter, served with soft-boiled eggs splashed with dark soy sauce and white pepper, paired with local coffee (Kopi).
